SLIDE 02 — PROBLEM : "Skills are everywhere. The right connection isn't."
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCRIPT:
"Problem kya hai? Aaj kal skills ki kami nahi hai — YouTube pe, Instagram pe,
har jagah log kuch na kuch sikha rahe hain.

Lekin dikkat ye hai:

Pehla — Scattered discovery. Aapko Python wala chahiye, lekin group chats aur
random posts me aapko sahi banda dhoondhna mushkil hai. Koi central jagah nahi
jahan aap skill, language ya outcome se search kar sako.

Dusra — Non-reciprocal wants. Example dekho — Asha Python sikhati hai aur Guitar
seekhna chahti hai. Cara Design sikhati hai aur Python seekhna chahti hai. Ye
direct swap nahi banega, kyunki wants match nahi kar rahe. 'A ≠ C' — ye mismatch
bahut common hai.

Teesra — Messy agreement. Alag time zones, alag languages, 15 se 120 minutes
tak ke durations — bina conflict check ke lessons miss ho jate hain.

Chautha — No proof of learning. Sirf attendance se pata nahi chalta ki banda
ne actually kya banaya, kya seekha.

Isiliye humne SkillLoop banaya — ek single place jahan ye sab solve ho."

[Transition: Next slide]

SLIDE 03 — SOLUTION : "One place to discover, agree, learn and get recognized"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCRIPT:
"Solution simple hai — hamara tagline hai: 'Share something you know, learn
something you need — and keep a record.'

SkillLoop pe 3 tareeke se lesson arrange hota hai:

1. Free session — bina credits ya swap ke, bas sikhane ke liye.
2. Direct skill swap — do log, do alag lessons. Asha Ben ko Python sikhayegi,
   Ben Asha ko Guitar — do alag time slots.
3. Paid mentorship — demo credits se. Har naya user ko 100 demo credits milte
   hain, ye real money nahi hai, sirf simulation hai, 15-minute hold ke saath.

Aur jo sabse unique hai — Trade Circles. Jab do logon ka match nahi banta, to
teen logon ka loop banta hai. Asha → Cara (Python), Cara → Ben (Design), Ben → Asha
(Guitar). Teen alag 1-to-1 lessons, ek group call nahi.

Flow dekho: Publish skill → Discover → Request & consent → Learn & confirm →
Badge + XP earn karo. Aur naya kya hai? Badges aur Leaderboard.

Har action pe badge milta hai — 12 badges, alag rarity — common, rare, epic,
legendary. Aur Leaderboard pe live ranks — Last 7 days, Weekly (Monday to Sunday)
aur Monthly — teeno windows. Isse motivation aata hai, shaming nahi."

[Transition: Next slide]

SLIDE 04 — HOW IT WORKS + TERMINOLOGY : "From browser to database — no magic"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCRIPT:
"Ab thoda technical, lekin simple language me — How it works?

Hamara stack ek hi full-stack app hai: Browser (React 19 + TypeScript, desktop-
first but responsive), phir Next.js API (Zod validation, ownership check, time
slot conflict check), aur phir Database (local pe SQLite, production pe
PostgreSQL, transactions ke saath, chat polling se).

Koi AI magic nahi, koi video call ka jhooth nahi — sab rule-based aur explainable.

Terminology samajh lo, taaki demo me confuse na ho:

- Skill listing — Title, Category (Dev, Design, Languages, Music, Business etc),
  Level, Teaching language, Duration 15-120 min, Online/In-person, Tags 1-8,
  Outcome 10-300 characters. Proof link optional hota hai.

- Booking — Status hota hai: pending → countered → awaiting-payment (15 min) →
  scheduled → completed / declined / cancelled / expired. Reschedule bhi kar sakte
  ho agar attendance confirm nahi hua.

- Trade Circle — 3 alag log, sab online, same language, exact tag-want match.
  1/3, 2/3, 3/3 consent — teeno agree karenge tabhi 3 bookings active hongi.

- Badge — 12 badges, har ek ka alag kaam. Example: Trailblazer (first skill),
  First Spark (first session). Har badge ka XP 50 se 250 tak.

- Leaderboard — Points system: Completed lesson +40 (+15 trade, +20 circle),
  Skill share +25, 5-star review +50, Message +2 (max 20), Post +15. Points har
  window me reset, lekin XP hamesha rahega.

- XP aur Level — XP = saare earned badges ka sum. Level = floor(XP/200)+1.
  Simple.

Callout dekho: Publish → Discover → Request → Agree → Learn → Recognize — yehi
hamara loop hai."

SLIDE 06 — LEADERBOARD : "Climb by learning, teaching and helping others"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCRIPT:
"Next, Leaderboard — kyunki healthy competition bhi zaroori hai.

Hamare paas 3 windows hai, upar segmented control me dekho:

- Last 7 days — rolling 7 din, live.
- Weekly — Monday se Sunday, UTC, har Monday reset.
- Monthly — Calendar month, har 1st ko reset.

Kaise rank hota hai? Points se. Points kaise milte hain — slide ke neeche dekho:

Completed lesson +40, agar trade hai to +15 extra, circle hai to +20 extra.
Skill share +25, 5-star review +50, Message +2 (max 20 taki spam na ho), Post +15.
Points har window me reset ho jate hain, lekin XP aur badges hamesha rahenge —
isliye koi permanent shaming nahi, har week naya chance.

Podium dekho — #2, #1, #3. #1 pe crown hai. Asha 320 points, Ben 286, Cara 264 —
ye example data hai, live workspace me aapka rank alag hoga.

Neeche table me #4 pe 'You' dikh raha hai — 180 points, 3 sessions, Level 3.
Aapka rank hamesha highlight rahega, chahe top 3 me na ho.

Sorting: Points → XP → Name. Aur ye sirf demo workspace ka ranking hai, global
ranking nahi — kyunki har naya account apna isolated workspace banata hai.

Ye feature kyun? Kyunki jab aap dekhte ho ki last 7 din me Asha ne 3 sessions
kiye, to aapko bhi lagta hai — 'Mujhe bhi karna chahiye.' Motivation without
tuition."

SLIDE 07 — KEY FEATURES : "What actually works today"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCRIPT:
"Ab quick overview — Kya actually kaam karta hai aaj, whitepaper ke hisaab se.

8 boxes dekho:

1. Account & profile — 18+ age check, password 10-128 chars, demo inbox me 6-digit
   code (5 min expiry, no real email/SMS), avatar 512KB, timezone, languages,
   wants 1-10.

2. Skill management — Create, edit, pause, reuse. Har user max 20 skills. 'Other'
   category bhi hai. Tags 1-8, soft delete — completed history safe rahega.

3. Discovery — Title, description, tag, @username se search. Filters: category,
   language, offer type, delivery. Saved skills bhi.

4. Bookings — Free, paid (100 credits start), trade. States jo pehle bataye,
   reschedule bhi possible agar attendance nahi hua.

5. Trade Circles — Rule-based exact match, unanimous consent, 3 bookings.

6. Chat & records — Polling chat, 5 sec state refresh, 3 sec chat refresh.
   500 msgs per convo, last 100 dikhte hain. Attendance (dono confirm), evidence
   (learner), feedback (teacher), reviews (1-5 stars, 5-700 chars).

7. Badges NEW — 12 badges, XP, Level floor(XP/200)+1, profile aur badges page
   pe visible.

8. Leaderboard NEW — Weekly / Monthly / Last 7 days, podium + table + your rank.

Limits bhi batana zaroori hai: Posts 150, circles 30, bookings 250 per workspace.
Kya nahi hai? Real money, video call, AI matching, escrow, moderation panel —
ye sab nahi hai, aur hum openly batate hain."

TIPS FOR PRESENTER (Hinglish me hi)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Har slide pe 45-60 sec max rukna, zyada text mat padhna — visual ko point
   karo.
2. Badges slide pe ek badge ka example live dikhao — 'Trailblazer kaise milta
   hai' — Share a skill pe click karo.

   bahut log confuse hote hain.
4. Demo ke time Asha (Python), Ben (Guitar), Cara (Design) wala loop use karo —
   sabse easy demo hai.
5. Agar judge puche 'Ye real hai?' — bolo 'Demo workspace, isolated, credits
   fictional, no real payment, no AI — sab whitepaper me clearly mentioned hai.'
6. Ending me hamesha localhost ka link bolo aur 'Thank you' ke baad 2 sec pause lo.
