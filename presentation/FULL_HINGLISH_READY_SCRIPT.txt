A: "Real payment ka scope aur risk bada hai, demo me workflow dikhana tha — simulation, escrow/withdraw nahi, whitepaper me clearly mentioned."

A: "3-way beyond 2-way, unanimous consent, proof beyond attendance, badges+windows motivation — sab rule-based, no hype."
